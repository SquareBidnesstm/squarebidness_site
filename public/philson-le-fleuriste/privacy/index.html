Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 29, 2026  ·  Square Bidness Holdings, Inc.

This Privacy Policy describes how Philson Le Fleuriste, operated by Square Bidness Holdings, Inc. ("we," "us," or "our"), collects, uses, and protects information you provide when you use our website or submit a floral request.

Information We Collect

When you submit a floral request or deposit form, we collect your name, phone number, email address, event details, and any other information you voluntarily provide. We also collect standard web analytics data such as browser type and page visits.

SMS Messaging

By providing your phone number and submitting a request form on our website, you consent to receive SMS text messages from Philson Le Fleuriste at the number you provided. These messages may include order confirmations, deposit notifications, event reminders, and customer care communications.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

We do not share your mobile phone number with third parties for their own marketing purposes. Your number is used solely to communicate with you about your floral order or inquiry.

To opt out, reply STOP to any message. To get help, reply HELP or contact us at marcusbuckner@squarebidness.com.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to process your floral requests, send order confirmations and deposit links, communicate event details, and improve our services. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Data Sharing

We may share your information with service providers who assist us in operating our business (such as payment processors and communication platforms) under confidentiality agreements. We do not share your mobile number with third parties for marketing purposes.

Payment Information

All payments are processed through Stripe. We do not store your credit card or bank account details. Stripe's privacy policy governs the handling of your payment information.

Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as necessary to fulfill your order and comply with legal obligations. You may request deletion of your data by contacting us.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us at marcusbuckner@squarebidness.com. Louisiana residents may have additional rights under applicable state law.
