The Origin

Good Friday, 2025.
Highway 190. Tire Off.

Marcus D. Buckner is a U.S. Army veteran from Tangipahoa Parish, Louisiana. On Good Friday 2025, a tire came off his grandfather's GMC on Highway 190. He had just returned from Stripe Sessions in San Francisco — his first time seeing what software could actually do at scale.

He went home and started coding. No CS degree. No investors. No team. Eighteen months later: nine live platforms, two server nodes, and a holding company building the technology infrastructure rural Louisiana never had.

Square Bidness Holdings is not a pivot or a side project. It is a full-stack holding company — verticals, infrastructure, certifications — built in the place everyone else skipped.

"The frontier in AI is rural. We didn't come to ask permission to be in this conversation. It already started here without them."
Marcus D. Buckner · Managing Director
